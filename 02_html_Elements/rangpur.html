Rangpur

Rangpur is a city in northern Bangladesh. It is the capital of Rangpur Division and Rangpur District. The city is located on the banks of the Ghaghat River, which flows through the region. Rangpur has a rich history and cultural heritage, with several historical landmarks and monuments.

The city is known for its vibrant markets, traditional crafts, and delicious local cuisine. Rangpur is also home to several educational institutions, including universities and colleges, making it an important center for education in the region.

Rangpur experiences a tropical climate, with hot summers and mild winters. The city is well-connected by road and rail, making it easily accessible from other parts of Bangladesh.
